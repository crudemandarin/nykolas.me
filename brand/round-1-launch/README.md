# NYKO · P0 brand extensions (brief §3 A–D, items 1–15)

Everything in this folder is generated by `../gen.py` (geometry helpers in `../geom.py`).
Regenerate instead of hand-editing:

```sh
cd /home/claude/graphics
npm install            # @fontsource fonts, used only for the share card
python3 gen.py         # all outputs (add --no-chrome to skip og-card + contact sheet)
```

Needs `skia-pathops` (pip), `rsvg-convert`, ImageMagick `convert` (for the .ico), and `google-chrome`.
Geometry is read at run time from the existing marks and never redrawn by eye:

- The rocket hull planes and the N⅄KO wordmark path come from `nyko-animated.svg`.
- The single-plume flame comes from `nyko-1bit.svg`.

All booleans (occlusion gaps, knock-outs, 1-bit unions) are exact path operations, so the SVGs contain
only `<path>` elements. They have no masks, rasters, fonts, gradients or opacity.
Intermediate sources (app-icon SVGs, `og-card.html`, `contact-sheet.html`) are in `../build/`.

**Flight Mode art** has its own script, which needs only `skia-pathops`: `python3 gen_flight.py`.
It writes `nyko-laser-pod.svg`, the three HUD glyphs (see C), the game-over plate `nyko-flight-los.svg` (the climb trajectory breaks and the rocket tumbles, engines out, in the 404 art's language) and `qa/flight-mode-qa.svg`, which shows the pod on the rocket at 1× and 0.4× and the glyphs at 24/16px on Night and Day.
It also copies the pod and the plate to `public/brand/` and the glyphs to `public/brand/ui/`.
The pod is hardware art that the hero canvas draws inside the rocket's transform. Like `public/brand/nyko-ufo.svg`, it uses the rocket's gradient and strokes, not the single-path rules above.
The deploy linkage, the anchors (`mount`, `muzzle`) and the clearances are in the pod's header comment.

Palette: ink `#1E2233` · hull-shadow `#525C7A` · hull-mid `#9AA4BE` · hull-light `#C9CFDE` ·
hull-xlight `#DDE2EC` · deep-indigo `#2A3170` · brand-blue `#3238C9` · ember `#FF4A22` ·
amber `#FFB300`. Grounds: Night `#101322`, Day `#F4F5F9`.

## C · UI glyphs

All glyphs use a 24px grid with 2px padding: every glyph's bounds are asserted to lie inside 2–22.
Each is one `<path fill="currentColor">` with the strokes already expanded, so CSS `color` tints it.

- **Stroke:** 1.5, with 1px for hairline detail. Caps are butt and joins are mitred (hardware corner logic) throughout.
- **Lattice:** straight strokes sit on the lattice 0.75 + 1.5k (… 3.75, 5.25 … 20.25). At 16px their centres fall on pixel centres, so lines render as exactly 1px.
- **Checked at:** 16px and 24px on both grounds (contact sheet).

| `nyko-ui-arrow-up-right.svg` | External link. Also the recommended link-out for GitHub and LinkedIn (see D). |

## D · Comms marks (24px, `currentColor`)

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `nyko-ui-email.svg` | Email link. |
| `nyko-ui-document.svg` | Résumé or document: a page with a folded corner and three text rules. |
| `nyko-ui-antenna.svg` | Generic "transmission": a solid parabolic dish (boresight at 45°) with a feed arm, pedestal, base plate and one signal arc. |

**GitHub and LinkedIn: deliberately not drawn.** Both brands' guidelines require their official marks
unaltered, so redrawing them on our grid is off the table. The recommendation is brief §15 option (b):
label those links in words ("GitHub", "LinkedIn") and pair them with the neutral `nyko-ui-arrow-up-right.svg`
link-out glyph. That keeps one icon language and avoids placing third-party logos.
If logos are required later, use the official single-colour files from each brand's press kit,
optically sized to match a 24px glyph.

## Contact sheet

`contact-sheet.png` (2640px wide) shows every output on Night (left) and Day (right), rendered by Chrome:
- wordmarks and lockups at 1× (40 and 32px tall) and 2×;
- the compact mark;
- the favicons at 1× plus an 8× nearest-neighbour zoom of the 16px raster;
- the pinned tab, the app icons at 1×, and the share card at 1×;
- all 14 glyphs at 48, 24 and 16px.

Single-ink 1-bit art is shown inverted on Night. Items made for the other ground are labelled as such.

## Contrast (WCAG ratio vs Night `#101322` / Day `#F4F5F9`)

ember 5.49 / 3.08 · amber 10.28 / 1.65 · ink 1.17 / 14.47 · indigo 1.56 / 10.88 ·
hull-shadow 2.79 / 6.08 · hull-mid 7.40 / 2.29 · hull-light 11.83 / 1.43.

This is why the compact mark and lockup ship as separate Night and Day files. On Day the amber orb is
given an ink ring so it clears 3:1. The amber flame is the one sub-3:1 element on Day; it is decorative,
and the silhouette carries the meaning.
